### What Nuance Labs actually is

Seattle AI research company founded in 2025 by ex-Apple PhDs (CEO Fangchang Ma, co-founder Edward Zhang), building photorealistic real-time AI avatars with emotional intelligence. The technical thesis is full-duplex: a system that listens and speaks simultaneously, perceives emotion in real time, and drives a face that reflects it, at sub-500ms latency against a current-state 2-5 seconds. They are training their own omni foundation models rather than assembling a speech-to-speech pipeline.

Funding: $10M seed led by Accel with Lightspeed and South Park Commons (September 2025), followed by an SEC Form D filed 2026-04-27 for approximately $60.8M, listing Ma as CEO. The JD names Accel, Lightspeed, South Park Commons and NVIDIA. Total raised is roughly $70M. This is a credible, well-capitalized seed-to-Series-A research startup, not a vaporware shop.

### What this role actually is

The JD is unambiguous once you read past the title. "Human evaluation" here means **human-subjects research on product experience**, not model evaluation engineering. The evidence:

- The qualification bar is "5+ years designing and running human-subjects research in industry or academia (UX research, HCI, experimental psychology, behavioral science, or a related field)."
- The methods named are interviews, think-alouds, diary studies, longitudinal panels, ethnography, contextual inquiry, field work, survey and psychometric design.
- The statistics named are agreement and reliability statistics for rating and pairwise-comparison data: Cohen's kappa, Krippendorff's alpha.
- The operational scope is participant panels, rater training and calibration, and annotation tooling.

The organizing question is "does this avatar feel human," and the deliverable is a defensible instrument for measuring that with people. Exactly one of the five listed responsibilities touches the candidate's territory: "Calibrate automated and model-based metrics (including LLM-as-judge) against human judgment." That is one bullet out of five, and even that bullet frames human judgment as the reference standard the model-based metric is validated against, which is the UXR side of the interface.

Reading this posting as an evals role because the word "evaluation" is in the title would be the central error available here, and it is worth naming plainly.
