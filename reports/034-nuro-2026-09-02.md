### Gap analysis and mitigation

**1. Automated hill climbing (stage 3 of the eval pipeline).**
Hard blocker? No, but it is the closest thing to one, because the JD names owning all three stages as "the strongest signal for this role". Adjacent experience: the BMO RL environments work and the RLinf flow-matching integration are both about making an objective searchable rather than hand-tuned; boundary resampling at BEHAVIOR-1K searched a data strategy. Portfolio proof: none directly. Mitigation: do not claim it. Say stages 1 and 2 have been owned for a real system, that stage 3 is the reason this role is interesting, and then be specific about what a first hill-climbing loop would look like on one BMO workflow. The JD itself warns that stage 3 done on unsound stages 1 and 2 "optimizes hard against a metric that means nothing", which is exactly the failure mode his bias-discovery work is about. Turning the gap into the reason he is credible on the guardrail is honest and strong.

**2. Production-scale engineering.**
Hard blocker? No. The JD asks for "a real engineering background ... able to stand up the infrastructure your own experiment needs", which is a self-sufficiency bar, not a distributed-systems bar. Note what the posting does *not* ask for: no Kubernetes, no multi-node training, no CUDA. It explicitly partitions that away: "You will work in close partnership with the engineer who builds the platform this runs on: they make it run safely at scale, you determine what it should be doing and whether it worked." That partition is the specific justification for waiving this one (Calibration Rule 4), and it is the sharpest difference from Scale AI #008 (3.3) and Cursor #025 (3.6), where infra *was* the primary requirement.

**3. Online experimentation and causal inference.**
Hard blocker? No, Bonus Points only. Mitigation: name it as a gap in interview and pair it with the variance discipline that is evidenced (bounded-effect finding, matched-hyperparameter comparisons, rejected rival hypotheses). Asking a good question about their offline-to-online correlation is worth more here than pretending.

**4. Degree in progress against an on-site role.**
Hard blocker? No, and not a credential issue: the JD accepts equivalent research experience. It is a logistics question (M.Eng to April 2027, Mountain View HQ) and it will be asked. Have a flat answer ready. Do not volunteer it as a problem.

**5. Domain distance.**
The everyday work is internal engineering-productivity agents, not embodied manipulation. The driving-data VLM post-training half is real robotics work but it is perception and driving, not the manipulation and VLA-control lane. Not a gap in qualification; it is a preference question worth being honest about in Block C.

## C) Level and Strategy

### JD level versus natural level

The posting carries no level in the title and no YoE floor. The band ($193,930 to $352,290 base) is wide enough to span roughly three levels, and the JD says so directly: "In the event that you are considered for a different level, a higher or lower pay range would apply." Against Levels.fyi, Nuro Research Scientist runs $249K to $374K total comp with a $289K median, which places the mid-band at a genuinely senior scope.

His natural level here is the lower-middle of that band. Wall-clock: BMO from Sep 2025 and Merlyn from Aug 2025, concurrent, roughly one year, plus the Epineuron co-op (May 2021 to Aug 2022). The research output is well beyond one year of typical output, but the *ownership surface* the top of this band implies (setting the statistical standard a team accepts changes on, and putting a defensible number on platform value in front of the CEO) is a stretch.

### Screen risk, stated plainly

Per Calibration Rule 3, no hand-waving. There is no mechanical filter to fail here: no YoE dropdown, no PhD gate, no publication gate. That is the good news, and it is unusual for this pipeline. The risk is different in kind:

- **Level scoping, not rejection.** The most likely negative outcome is not a no, it is an offer scoped near $194K to $230K base rather than mid-band. That still clears the $180K floor and is close to the $250K robotics-startup target once bonus and equity are counted, but it is well under the $300K to $500K frontier-lab aim.
- **A taste bar in place of a credential bar.** The JD is unusually opinionated ("Frontier models are fungible", "most of it is noise", "opinions about which recent directions are overrated"). Whoever wrote it will screen on whether the candidate has a real, defensible, unpopular opinion. He does: the miscalibrated-baseline argument. Résumé bullets alone will not carry this; the argument has to be visible in the application.
- **Small team, few seats.** "Small team, no established playbook" means low headcount and high per-req selectivity even though the brand filter is weak. Volume of applicants is likely far lower than at OpenAI or Anthropic, which is the whole attainability case.
- **Recruiter-stage risk is mild.** A generalist screener sees an in-progress M.Eng and about one year of full-time experience against a band topping $352K. Mitigate by making the artifacts do the work early: the technical report, the LessWrong post, and the RLinf merge are all externally verifiable in one click.

### Selling seniority without inflating it

Lead with owned scope, not tenure. Three true statements carry it:

1. "Found a systematic misalignment in a tool serving $200B+ AUM, by building the eval pipeline that made it visible." That is stages 1 and 2 owned end to end on a real production system, which is the exact thing the JD calls the strongest signal.
2. "Established that a widely cited VLA baseline is miscalibrated, and showed the fix is the training recipe rather than the architecture." That is literature judgment converted into an experiment, which is the second most emphasised trait.
3. "Co-founded a three-person research collective that placed 8th in Stanford's BEHAVIOR-1K Challenge and shipped the write-up." That is ownership without a manager.

Per the Merlyn restraint rule, pick one or two of these per artifact rather than stacking all three. For this role, 1 and 2 are the pair. Do not lead with the BMO greeter robot, and do not describe Merlyn as anything beyond a self-organized collective.

### If they downlevel

Accept a level, negotiate the number and the scope, and do it in that order. Script from the profile, adapted:

> "I'm comparing against US research engineering roles in the $300K range. What draws me to this one is that it's the only posting I've seen that wants the eval pipeline and the post-training in the same person. I'm flexible on level. On base, I'm targeting the middle of the posted band, and I'd rather talk about what I own in the first two quarters than about the title."

If the number lands low, trade for the thing that compounds: ownership of the first end-to-end hill-climbing workflow (the JD names it as a first-two-quarters deliverable) and access to the post-training loop with the labeling workforce. Those are the two assets here that no other req in this pipeline offers.

**On domain honesty:** if asked whether he wants autonomous driving, the true answer is that the eval half is the North Star and the post-training half is the robotics work, and internal-developer-agents is the vehicle rather than the draw. Say a version of that. This team's entire pitch is integrity about what the numbers support; performing enthusiasm for the domain would read as exactly the failure mode they are hiring against.

### Recommended case study

**"Take one high-volume internal workflow from unmeasured to automatically hill-climbing."** This is a first-two-quarters deliverable in the posting, so proposing it unprompted shows he read carefully. Structure it in their three stages:

1. **Where ground truth comes from.** Human accept, reject and edit signal captured in the tools engineers already use, plus mined production traces. Say out loud which of those is biased and how: accept and reject signal is censored by what the agent was willing to attempt.
2. **Loop construction.** Establish the noise floor before any acceptance threshold. Build the task suite from the real distribution of requests rather than the requests that are easy to score, and state where a model-based judge is trustworthy versus where it launders the assumption being tested.
