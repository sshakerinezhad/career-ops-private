## C) Level and Strategy

**JD level vs natural level.** The title carries no prefix, but the requirement block describes a mid-to-senior IC: 3+ years of framework work, a PhD or MS-plus-2-years, a track record of shipping policies onto physical robots, and mentoring. Shayan's natural level on research substance is close to this. His natural level on wall-clock tenure is not. There is a sibling requisition in the scan history, "Senior Reinforcement Learning Engineer" (job 5813673004, seen 2026-07-06), which suggests this unprefixed posting is deliberately the more junior of the two. That is a point in its favor: this is the right one of the pair to target.

**Screen risk, stated plainly (Calibration Rule 3).** Two fields will be evaluated mechanically before a human reads the résumé:

1. **Education and experience.** "PhD, or MS with 2+ years industry experience." The M.Eng is not conferred until April 2027 and BMO tenure is about one year. On a strict read, neither branch is satisfied today. A recruiter filtering on degree-complete plus years will drop this application.
2. **3+ years with RL frameworks and physics simulators.** Dated evidence begins around Sep 2024. On wall-clock this is roughly two years, and only about one of those is full-time-equivalent depth.

Neither of these is waivable by argument on a form. What makes them waivable in practice is external evidence that a screener recognizes: a top-10 finish in Stanford's BEHAVIOR-1K Challenge, a merged contribution to RLinf, and a written technical report. Those are the artifacts that let a hiring manager overrule a filter, and they only work if a human sees them. That is why the warm path is not optional here (Calibration Rule 7c).

**How to sell seniority without inflating.** The honest senior signal is scope, not tenure: co-founded a research collective, chose the research direction, ran the experiments, wrote the report, shipped the code upstream. Say that flat. Use the standing YoE answer of 2-4 on dropdowns per the ratified 2026-07-28 decision. Do not describe the M.Eng as a research masters and do not use "M.Eng candidate."

**If they downlevel.** Take it. A humanoid RL team at a company with roughly $1B raised and a hardware fleet is exactly the environment where the missing pieces (locomotion, whole-body control, deployable C++) get built. The scripted response: "I am optimizing for the problem, not the title. If the level reflects where I start rather than where I finish, that works. What I would want to understand is what the path to the senior scope looks like on this team." Then negotiate comp on the base, since the level concession is already given.

**Stretch label.** This is a **stretch** application on the experience gates and a solid one on content. It should not crowd out the more attainable, assessment-first robotics targets in the same batch.
